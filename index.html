We'll also have an auction booth and a trading table. Come along and meet other LPS lovers on August 9th!

Frequently asked questions

Is this event suitable for young children?

Absolutely! LPSMinicon is a family-friendly event designed with kids of all ages in mind. Children under 5 are free with a paying adult.

Can I bring items to trade or sell?

Attendees are welcome to bring items to trade at the trading table. For the auction booth, please apply for a vendor table in the Vendor & Table Info section above.

Are tickets refundable?

Tickets may be transferred to another person but are non-refundable after purchase.

Is there food available at the venue?

Yes! The event opens at noon with an Order & Eat period before the program begins. Food options will be available throughout the event.

Is cosplay/costume welcome?

Of course! Costumes and creative outfits inspired by LPS and related fandoms are warmly encouraged. All costumes must be family-appropriate.
