01

Name treatment

LPSMinicon
Do Always write as "LPSMinicon" — one word, no spaces. "LPS" in pink, "Minicon" in blue when color is available.
Do In plain text (emails, documents), write "LPSMinicon" with capital L, P, S, and M.
Don't Never write "LPS Minicon," "Lps Minicon," "lpsminicon," or "LPS Mini Con."
Don't Never apply the two-color treatment in a single solid color context — use navy instead.

05

Voice & tone

LPSMinicon speaks to kids and their families. The voice is warm, excited, and inclusive — never condescending, never too corporate. Friendly but organized.

Write like this
  • Clear, short sentences
  • Active voice ("Join us for Bingo!")
  • Warm and welcoming ("Everyone's invited")
  • Specific times and locations
  • Exclamation points used sparingly for real excitement
Avoid this
  • Jargon or overly formal language
  • Vague timing ("sometime in the afternoon")
  • Talking down to younger attendees
  • All-caps for emphasis
  • Excessive exclamation points!!!!
